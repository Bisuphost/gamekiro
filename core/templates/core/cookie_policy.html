Cookie Policy

Last updated: September 13, 2026

This page explains what cookies GameKiro uses and why. We keep this deliberately short, because we keep our cookie use deliberately minimal.

Cookies we use

GameKiro sets exactly two cookies, and both are strictly necessary for the Service to work — we don't use any advertising, analytics, or tracking cookies.

Cookie Purpose Type
sessionid Keeps you logged in between page visits. Strictly necessary
csrftoken Protects form submissions against cross-site request forgery. Strictly necessary

Why we don't show a cookie consent banner

Under laws like the EU/UK ePrivacy rules, cookies that are strictly necessary for a service you've actively requested (like staying logged in, or securing a form you're submitting) don't require an opt-in consent banner — only disclosure, which is what this page provides. If we ever add cookies that aren't strictly necessary (for example, analytics or advertising), we will add a consent banner before doing so and update this policy first.

Third-party resources

Loading Google Fonts and the htmx JavaScript library (from a public CDN) causes your browser to make direct requests to those providers, which may see your IP address as a normal part of serving those files. Neither is used by us for tracking, and neither sets a cookie on GameKiro's behalf.

Managing cookies in your browser

You can clear or block cookies at any time through your browser settings. Since our two cookies are required for login and secure form submission, blocking them will prevent you from staying signed in to GameKiro.
